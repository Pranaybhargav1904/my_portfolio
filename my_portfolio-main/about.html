About Me

From Backend Architecture to AI-Powered Products

I'm a Full Stack Developer with 5+ years of experience building enterprise backend systems and web applications. My work spans Java/Spring Boot microservices, React and Next.js frontends, REST APIs and GraphQL, and multi-database environments including PostgreSQL, Oracle DB, MySQL, and SQL Server.

I'm most energized by problems at the intersection of backend reliability, data integrity, and clean developer experience — and I've been increasingly working AI integrations (OpenAI, Groq) into production workflows.
